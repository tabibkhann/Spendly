Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filtering and search. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. You may request a full export or permanent deletion of your data at any time by contacting us through the app.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Any external libraries used (such as fonts) are loaded from trusted providers and do not receive your personal or financial data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
